Fishing

Cast a line at Littlebug Town’s Pond. Hook a fish, reel it in and keep it. Now and then you might pull up a boot.

How to fishWhat you can catch

How to fish

  • Choose World → Pond. Your Tama needs to be awake, healthy and past the baby stage.
  • Press C to cast. Then wait for a bite; the wait is between 2 and 6 seconds.
  • The bobber twitches now and then. Those are only nibbles; pressing C on one is too soon and scares the fish off.
  • When the bobber goes under and BITE! appears, press C quickly (within 0.9 seconds) to hook the fish.
  • Reel it in: a bar along the bottom shows the fish and your reel zone. Use A and B to keep the fish inside the zone. The line above the bar fills while it is inside and empties while it is outside.
  • Fill the line to land the catch; let it empty and the fish gets away. Press C to cast again, or D to leave.

What you can catch

Everything you land goes into Games → Items. Rarer fish tend to be faster and harder to keep in the zone.
