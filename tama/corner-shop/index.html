Corner Shop

The shop in Littlebug Town. It sells four items at a time and restocks three times a day.

Going shoppingBuyingWhat the shop can sellNew stock

Going shopping

ButtonWhat it does
CGo in past the clerk; buy the item on screen
APrevious item on the shelf
BNext item on the shelf
DLeave the shop
  • Choose World, then Corner Shop. Your Tama needs to be a child or older, and awake.
  • The top of the screen shows your points. Below that is the item’s name, its picture, and its price.

Buying

  • Press C to buy the item on screen. The clerk thanks you and the item goes into Food or Items.
  • If you don’t have enough points, the shop says NO POINTS and nothing is spent.
  • You can own up to 99 of each item; after that the shop says FULL.

What the shop can sell

ItemPriceKindDescription

Juice40 pointsFoodA tall glass of fizzy fruit juice with a straw. Sold at the Corner Shop.
Enrg Drink60 pointsFoodA fizzy can that perks your Tama up. Its energy can go past the most (boosted energy), but it adds a little stress. Sold at the Corner Shop.

New stock

  • The shelf changes at midnight, 3 p.m. and 7 p.m. (on your Tama’s clock).
  • Each shelf is four different things chosen at random from the list above.
  • Time stands still for your Tama while you shop.
