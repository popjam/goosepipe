Durations are time spent in each stage, including sleep and offline time. Mametchi and Maskutchi are alternative adult paths.

Which adult will you meet?

  • At the end of the teen stage, Mametchi requires at most 2 lifetime care mistakes and at least 0 training.
  • Otherwise, your Tama becomes Maskutchi.
  • Evolution keeps the individual pet’s needs and identity. Weight becomes at least the new form’s initial weight, within global limits.
  • Both adult paths eventually reach Bill, used here as the elder form. This is this game’s lifecycle, not a recreation of original Tamagotchi rules.

The next generation

  • When the elder stage ends, the lifetime finishes and care stops.
  • Press C / N at home to begin a new egg. The generation number increases.
  • The game retains the last four lifetime records, but the new Library → Family page is not accessible yet.
  • A long absence never starts another generation automatically.
