What decides the path

  • At the end of the teen stage, good care leads to Mametchi: at most 2 care mistakes in your Tama’s whole life, and at least 0 training.
  • Otherwise your Tama becomes Maskutchi.
  • Every adult grows into Bill, the elder.
  • Growing up keeps your Tama’s needs, name and identity. Its weight becomes at least the new form’s starting weight.

Growing up and games

  • Each new stage can bring new games, given at random. See the games room for how many each stage gets.
  • Time stands still for your Tama during a game, so it never grows up in the middle of one. It happens as soon as the game ends.

The next generation

  • When the elder stage ends, the lifetime finishes and care stops.
  • Press C at home to begin a new egg. The generation number goes up.
  • A new generation starts fresh: no games, no points and only the starting items. Your settings stay.
  • The game keeps the last four lifetimes, though the Library → Family page is not open yet.
  • A long absence never starts another generation by itself.
