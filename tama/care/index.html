Attention & good care

The Care page tells you what your Tama needs. Clear the need to clear its alert.

What triggers a call?

NeedTriggerTry this
HungryInternal hunger ≥ 80 (displayed fullness ≤ 20)Give a meal
UnhappyHappiness ≤ 20Give a snack; meet other needs before Pat
TiredEnergy ≤ 20Lights Out
DirtyAny poo on screenToilet
SickYour Tama is illRemove the cause, then Medicine

Care mistakes

  • A hungry, unhappy, tired or dirty alert left unresolved for 1800 seconds counts as one care mistake for that need.
  • The same unresolved alert does not keep adding mistakes. Clearing it resets its timer.
  • Sleep suppresses hungry, unhappy, tired and dirty attention alerts. Sickness remains visible.
  • Lifetime care mistakes help determine which adult your Tama becomes.

Pat & Time Out

ActionEffects
PatTraining +10, kindness +1, happiness +5
Time OutTraining +10, brains +1, happiness −5
BothEnergy −10, weight −1 g; 3600-second cooldown

Training requires an awake, healthy Tama with no unresolved need alerts and enough energy. Training caps at 100; skills cap at 999.
