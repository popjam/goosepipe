Sleep & Lights Out

Rest restores energy. Lights Out lets you put your Tama to bed or wake it up.

Bedtime routineWhile asleep

Bedtime routine

  • Select Lights Out to toggle sleep immediately. Select again on that page to wake up.
  • The current daily schedule sleeps at 21:00 and wakes at 7:00 when scheduled sleep is enabled.
  • A manual choice lasts until the next scheduled bedtime or wake time.
  • Energy recovers at 0.25 points per second, up to 100.

While asleep

  • Meals, training and starting games are unavailable.
  • Hunger, happiness, age and lifecycle time keep advancing. Sleep is not a pause button.
  • Poo and sickness handling continue. Routine need alerts are suppressed until awake.

Current scheduled-sleep setting: enabled.
