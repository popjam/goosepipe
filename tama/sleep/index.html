Sleep & Lights Out

Sleep restores energy. Lights Out puts your Tama to bed or wakes it up.

Bedtime routineWhile asleep

Bedtime routine

  • Select Lights Out to put your Tama to sleep. Select it again to wake it.
  • With scheduled sleep on, your Tama sleeps at 21:00 and wakes at 7:00.
  • A manual choice lasts until the next scheduled bedtime or wake time.
  • Energy recovers at 0.25 points per second, up to 100.

While asleep

  • Meals, training and starting games are unavailable.
  • Hunger, happiness, age and lifecycle time keep advancing.
  • Poo and sickness handling continue. Routine need alerts are suppressed until awake.

Scheduled sleep: enabled.
