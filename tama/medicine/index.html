Sickness & medicine

Fix the care problem first, then help your Tama recover.

How sickness startsTreatment, step by stepKeep the room clean

How sickness starts

  • Risk builds while an awake Tama’s internal hunger is at least 80, or while there are at least 3 piles of poo.
  • After 7200 continuous seconds at risk, the Tama becomes sick.
  • Removing both risk conditions resets the risk timer. There are no random flu strains or doctor diagnoses yet.

Treatment, step by step

  • Feed the Tama and clear enough poo to remove the risk conditions.
  • Choose Medicine → Medicine. If it says CARE FIRST, a risk condition remains.
  • Recovery takes 300 seconds. Additional doses do not stack while recovering.
  • The current illness system blocks training and game starts. It does not cause an early death.

Keep the room clean

  • Poo arrives every 3600 seconds, up to 8 piles.
  • Toilet clears all piles after a 1.2-second wipe.
