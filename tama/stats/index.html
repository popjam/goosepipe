The everyday needs

StatWhat it meansWhat changes it
HungryFive hearts. Full hearts mean fed; empty hearts mean hungry.Falls 1 point every 30 seconds. A normal meal restores 20 points.
HappinessFive hearts. Full hearts mean happy.Falls 1 point every 60 seconds. Snacks and Pat can improve it.
Energy0–100; not currently shown on a Stats page.Awake: loses 1 point every 60 seconds. Sleep: gains 0.25 points per second.
WeightYour Tama’s current weight in grams.Food adds weight. Training costs 1 g. Current global bounds: 5–99 g.
