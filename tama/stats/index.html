Health & stats

The heart icon opens Stats. Health shows your Tama’s needs, energy, skills and identity on seven pages. Personality is not open yet.

A new Tama starts with full energy. Growing up does not refill it; a stage with a lower most takes away the extra. In the day, a Tama that runs out of energy falls asleep, and wakes up when its energy is full. At night the sleep schedule decides. A Tama without the energy a game costs will not play it.

Lives

  • When stress reaches 99 with a life left, press C on the farewell screen. One life is used and the same Tama comes back with full hunger, happiness and energy and no stress.
  • Old age uses up every life: the life is over.
  • With no lives left, C begins a new egg.

A new Tama

  • A new Tama asks for the date, the time and its name before its egg starts. Time does not pass until they are given.
  • For the date and time, A and B change the underlined part, C moves on and D goes back.
  • For the name, A and B choose a letter, C adds it and D removes the last one. After Z comes the choice that finishes (C: DONE). A name has up to 15 letters.
  • The date becomes the birthday and the time sets the clock.
