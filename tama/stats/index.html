The everyday needs

StatWhat it meansWhat changes it
Hungry bar0–100. Full means fed; empty means hungry.Falls 1 point every 30 seconds. A normal meal restores 20 points.
Happiness0–100; expressed through Mood.Falls 1 point every 60 seconds. Snacks and Pat can improve it.
Energy0–100; not currently shown on a Stats page.Awake: loses 1 point every 60 seconds. Sleep: gains 0.25 points per second.
WeightYour Tama’s current weight in grams.Food adds weight. Training costs 1 g. Current global bounds: 5–99 g.

Three moods

MoodHappiness
Happy66–100
NeutralAbove 33 and below 66
Sad0–33

Mood currently reflects happiness only. Hunger and happiness still change during sleep; eggs and completed lives do not follow normal need decay.
