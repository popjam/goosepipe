Buttons & first steps

The Tama has four buttons: A, B, C and D.

ButtonsFirst stepsMore controls

A + DOpen Settings

First steps

  • A new Tama asks for the date, the time and its name: A and B change, C moves on or adds a letter, D goes back.
  • The egg hatches after 60 seconds of game time.
  • Open Stats → Health to check the Hungry and Happy hearts.
  • Choose Food → Meal and press C to feed when the hunger bar is low.
  • Use Toilet when poo appears. Your Tama naps by itself when tired; switch the lamp off for faster rest.
  • Games are given at random as your Tama grows: none as a baby, two by the child stage, four by the teen stage. Locked games stay hidden.

More controls

  • Meal, Snack and Items show one item per page: A and B browse, C feeds or uses it. Medicine actions happen as soon as you select them.
  • In Stats → Health, C moves forward through the seven pages.
  • Inside a game, buttons have game-specific actions. D always leaves the game.
  • Your Tama’s time keeps passing while you are away. Pausing at home with D stops the clock until you resume.
  • Games start with READY, SET, GO! and end with your points and a rating.
