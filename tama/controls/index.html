Meet the buttons

ButtonIn menus
APrevious icon, entry or page
BNext icon, entry or page
CSelect / confirm
DBack; at home, pause or resume

Your first few minutes

  • Wait for the egg to hatch. Its current hatch time is 60 seconds of game time.
  • Open Stats to check the Hungry and Happy hearts.
  • Choose Food → Meal when the hunger bar is low.
  • Use Toilet when poo appears. Choose Lights Out when your Tama needs rest.
  • Games unlock as your Tama grows. Jumping Rope and Climb open at child stage; Shape and Flag at teen stage.

A few useful habits

  • Selecting Food or Medicine actions performs them immediately.
  • Select cycles forward through the seven Stats pages.
  • Inside a game, buttons have game-specific actions. D always leaves the game.
  • Your Tama’s time keeps passing while you are away. Pausing at home with D stops the clock until you resume.
