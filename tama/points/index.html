Points

Win points by playing games, then spend them at the Corner Shop.

Earning pointsPoints for each gameSpending pointsKeeping track

Earning points

  • Games award points according to their own score tables. A Hurdles win gives 300 points.
  • When a game ends its score flashes, then its world record: the best score that game has ever had (NEW WORLD RECORD! when you have just beaten it). Then a pile of cash drops in front of your Tama (a boot if you earned none), the amount flashes, then the points you have now, and a word tells you how well you did.
  • Stopping a game with D gives no points.

Spending points

  • The Corner Shop in Littlebug Town sells four things at a time. Your points are shown at the top of the shop screen and the price underneath each item.
  • If you don’t have enough points, the shop tells you and nothing is spent.

Keeping track

  • Your points are on the sixth Stats page, next to Rep and Karma.
  • You can hold up to 999,999 points.
  • Points belong to your Tama: a new generation starts again with none.
