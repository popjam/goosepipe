Meals & snacks

Meals fill the hunger bar. Snacks lift happiness. Both add a little weight.

What’s on the menu?Choosing foodBefore you feed

What’s on the menu?

FoodMenuEffectDescription
SushiMeal+20 fullness, +1 gA tidy roll of rice and fish. A proper meal that fills your Tama right up.
BreadMeal+20 fullness, +1 gA soft, freshly baked loaf. Plain, but filling.
PuddingSnack+10 happiness, +2 gA wobbly caramel pudding. Sweet enough to lift any mood.
ConeSnack+10 happiness, +2 gIce cream in a crisp cone. Eat it before it melts!

Choosing food

  • Food → Meal and Food → Snack each open a list with one food per page.
  • A and B move to the previous or next food. The number in the bottom-right corner shows which page you are on.
  • C feeds the food on screen. Your Tama eats, then the list returns so you can feed again.
  • Basic foods never run out, so no amount is shown.

Before you feed

  • Wake a sleeping Tama before feeding it. Eggs and completed lives cannot eat.
  • Feeding is currently allowed when the hunger bar is already full; weight can still increase.
  • A character’s favourite or disliked food can adjust the happiness effect. See the character pages for assigned preferences.
