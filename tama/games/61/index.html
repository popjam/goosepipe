Games

Cup Game

Watch the ball go under a cup, follow the cups as they are shuffled on the table, then lift the right one. Five rounds, each faster than the last.

Playable

Can be drawn when your Tamagotchi becomes an adult or at any later stage. Cannot start while sleeping or sick.

On screen

Buttons

ButtonAction
AMove your hand to the cup on the left.
BMove your hand to the cup on the right.
CLift the cup your hand points at.
DStop the game (no points).

How to play

Each round the ball is shown under a cup, then the three cups swap places: 4 swaps in the first round, rising quickly to 20 very fast ones in the fifth, the hardest. Point at the cup you think hides the ball and lift it. The wrong cup ends the game. Score: rounds cleared.
