Games

Cup Game

Watch the ball go under a cup, follow the cups as they are shuffled on the table, then lift the right one. Six rounds, each harder than the last.

Playable

Can be drawn when your Tamagotchi becomes an adult or at any later stage. Cannot start while sleeping or sick.

On screen

Buttons

ButtonAction
AMove your hand to the cup on the left.
BMove your hand to the cup on the right.
CLift the cup your hand points at.
DPut your hand away to see the table (A, B or C brings it back). Pressed again: CANCEL? YES stops the game (no points).

How to play

Each round the ball is shown under a cup, then the three cups swap places: 4 swaps in the first round, rising quickly to 30 fast ones in the sixth, the hardest. Point at the cup you think hides the ball and lift it. The wrong cup ends the game. Score: rounds cleared.
