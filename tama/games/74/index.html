Games

Dominion

Build a deck against another Tamagotchi: play actions, buy treasure and better cards, and end with the most victory points. 10 of the 26 base-set kingdom cards each game.

Playable

Can be drawn when your Tamagotchi becomes an adult or at any later stage. Cannot start while sleeping or sick.

On screen

Buttons

ButtonAction
AMove back along your hand (action phase), over the supply piles (buy phase), or through a choice.
BMove on along your hand, over the supply piles, or through a choice.
CPlay the action card; buy the pile; in a choice, mark a card (or pick one). On the results: go on.
DGo on to buying; end your turn; in a choice of cards from your hand, done (or none). Held: CANCEL? YES stops the game (no points). A and B together: the card under the cursor in full.

How to play

Dominion for two, against a random adult. The supply: Copper, Silver, Gold, Estate, Duchy, Province (8 of each victory card), 10 Curses and 10 kingdom cards drawn at random from the 26 of the base set (Cellar, Chapel, Moat, Harbinger, Merchant, Vassal, Village, Workshop, Bureaucrat, Gardens, Militia, Moneylender, Poacher, Remodel, Smithy, Throne Room, Bandit, Council Room, Festival, Laboratory, Library, Market, Mine, Sentry, Witch, Artisan). Each player starts with 7 Coppers and 3 Estates and draws 5; who goes first is drawn. Your turn: play action cards while you have actions (1 to start; A / B along your hand, C plays; D goes on), then all your treasures are played and you buy (A / B over the supply, C buys while you have buys and coins; D or your last buy ends the turn); then everything is discarded and you draw 5 (your discard pile is shuffled when your deck runs out). With more than 5 cards your hand overlaps. Cards that ask something ask you: mark cards in your hand with C and finish with D, or pick a pile, an option or a card with A / B and C. Moat in your hand blocks attacks by itself. The other player plays its actions and buys a step at a time (1 s); when its attack needs your choice (Militia) you are asked. The game ends after a turn in which the Provinces run out or 3 supply piles are empty; the most victory points wins (a tie goes to whoever had fewer turns). Points for a win.
