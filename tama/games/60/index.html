Games

Snap

Take turns with another Tamagotchi laying cards on one pile. When the top two cards match, be first to call SNAP and the pile is yours.

Playable

Can be drawn when your Tamagotchi becomes an adult or at any later stage. Cannot start while sleeping or sick.

On screen

Buttons

ButtonAction
AThe previous card in your hand (hold to slide through).
BThe next card in your hand (hold to slide through).
CLay the card shown big. When the top two cards match: SNAP! (Calling SNAP when they don't gives the pile away.)
DStop the game (no points).

How to play

The deck is split between you and the opponent; each holds 5 cards, topped up from their own deck. Take turns laying one on the centre pile. When the top two cards have the same number, the first to call SNAP (C) takes the whole pile; the opponent calls it after about 0.7 seconds, so be quicker. Call SNAP when they don't match and the pile goes to the other player; the opponent slips up now and then too. When every card has been laid, whoever won more cards wins. Score: cards won, plus 10 for winning.
