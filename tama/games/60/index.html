Games

Snap

Cards turn over one by one onto a pile at the table. When the top two match, slap the pile before the other Tamagotchi does and it's yours.

Playable

Can be drawn when your Tamagotchi becomes an adult or at any later stage. Cannot start while sleeping or sick.

On screen

Buttons

ButtonAction
ASlap the pile.
BSlap the pile.
CSlap the pile.
DCANCEL? YES stops the game (no points); the game waits while it asks.

How to play

One deck lies face down beside the pile, and every 1 seconds its top card turns over onto the pile by itself. When the top two cards have the same number, the first to slap the pile (A, B or C) takes it all; the opponent slaps somewhere between 0.5 and 1.2 seconds, so be quicker. Slap when they don't match and the pile goes to the opponent (it slips up like that now and then too). When the deck runs out, whoever won more cards wins. Score: cards won, plus 10 for winning.
