Games

Snap

Cards turn over one by one onto a pile at the table. When the top two match, slap the pile before the other Tamagotchi does and it's yours.

Playable

Can be drawn when your Tamagotchi becomes an adult or at any later stage. Cannot start while sleeping or sick.

On screen

Buttons

ButtonAction
ASlap the pile.
BFake: your hand goes most of the way to the pile and pulls back. You can't slap until it is back.
CSlap the pile.
DCANCEL? YES stops the game (no points); the game waits while it asks.

How to play

One deck lies face down beside the pile, and every 1 seconds its top card turns over onto the pile by itself. When the top two cards have the same number, the first to slap the pile (A or C) takes it all; the opponent slaps somewhere between 0.5 and 1.2 seconds. Slap when they don't match and the pile goes to the opponent (it slips up like that now and then too). B fakes a slap: your hand goes most of the way to the pile and back (0.6 seconds, and no slapping until it is back); when the cards don't match it fools the opponent into slapping 30% of the time, and its wrong slap gives you the pile. The opponent fakes now and then too (15% of the cards), and can't slap until its hand is back. When the deck runs out, whoever won more cards wins. Score: cards won, plus 10 for winning.
