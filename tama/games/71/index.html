Games

Spit

A race with one deck: play a card one up or one down onto either pile before the other player does. First to get rid of every card wins.

Playable

Can be drawn when your Tamagotchi becomes an adult or at any later stage. Cannot start while sleeping or sick.

On screen

Buttons

ButtonAction
AMove along your hand (round from either end). With a card picked up: put it on the left pile. At SPIT: spit.
BMove along your hand (round from either end). With a card picked up: put it on the right pile. At SPIT: spit.
CPick up the card you are on; with a card picked up: put it back. At SPIT: spit.
DWith a card picked up: put it back. Otherwise put your hand away (A, B or C brings it back); pressed again: CANCEL? YES stops the game (no points).

How to play

Two piles in the middle and a spit pile beside each. A card goes on a pile one higher or one lower than its top: suits don't matter, and ace and king are next to each other. Your hand is up to 5 cards along the bottom, topped up from your stock as you play. Pick a card up with C, then A puts it on the left pile and B on the right, straight away. A card that fits neither freezes your hand for 3 seconds. The other player's hand carries its cards to the piles; change the pile it is going for before the card lands and it has to take the card back. When nobody can play, SPIT waits for you: A, B or C, and each spit pile turns a card onto its pile (the game starts this way too). First to get rid of all their cards wins. Points for a win.
