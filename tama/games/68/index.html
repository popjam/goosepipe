Games

Pig

Push your luck with a die against another Tamagotchi. Every roll adds to your turn's total, but the skull loses it all. Bank in time and be first to 50.

Playable

Can be drawn when your Tamagotchi becomes an adult or at any later stage. Cannot start while sleeping or sick.

On screen

Buttons

ButtonAction
ARoll the die: your hand shoots away and it tumbles across the table.
BBank your turn's total onto your score (the turn passes).
CNothing (A rolls, B banks).
DPut your hand away to see the table (A, B or C brings it back). Pressed again: CANCEL? YES stops the game (no points).

How to play

Who goes first is drawn at random. On your turn your open hand brings the die to the middle of the table and shakes it. A rolls: the die tumbles to a spot on the table and its number is added to your turn's total, then it comes back to your hand. B banks the total onto your score and the turn passes. Roll the skull and it's BUST: the turn's total is lost and the turn passes. The opponent usually banks at 20, sometimes earlier, sometimes it pushes for more, and always banks once that would win. First to 50 wins. Points for a win.
