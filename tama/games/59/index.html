Encyclopedia / Match
Games

Match

Take turns with another Tamagotchi flipping cards two at a time to find pairs. Find a pair and you go again.

Playable

Can be drawn when your Tamagotchi becomes an adult or at any later stage. Cannot start while sleeping or sick.

On screen

Buttons

ButtonAction
AMove your hand to the previous card. With two cards up: carry on.
BMove your hand to the next card. With two cards up: carry on.
CFlip the card your hand points at. With two cards up: carry on.
DPut your hand away to see the table (A, B or C brings it back). Pressed again: CANCEL? YES stops the game (no points).

How to play

Sixteen cards lie face down in a 4 by 4 grid: eight pairs, ace to 8. Flip two: a pair is yours and you go again; otherwise they turn back over and it is the other player's turn. After every two flips, yours or the opponent's, the cards stay up until you press A, B or C, until you are ready. The opponent remembers about 50% of the cards it sees. Score: one point for each pair you find, plus 4 for winning (more pairs than the opponent).
