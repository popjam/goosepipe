Games

Climb

Your Tamagotchi stands on a cloud while another drifts back and forth above it. Jump at the right moment to land on it and climb higher.

Playable

Child stage onwards, the first game you can play; the original TAMA form can also play. Cannot start while sleeping or sick.

On screen

Buttons

ButtonAction
AJump.
BJump.
CRestart after a miss or completion.
DCancel and return to the game list.

How to play

Land on 30 clouds to win. You land if your Tamagotchi is under the moving cloud when it reaches cloud height; otherwise it falls and the game ends. Each new cloud comes in from the opposite side, moves faster and changes size, mostly getting smaller. Waiting is safe: the cloud keeps sweeping until you jump.
