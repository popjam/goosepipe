Games

Connect 4

Take turns with another Tamagotchi dropping discs into an upright board. Four of yours in a row wins.

Playable

Can be drawn when your Tamagotchi becomes an adult or at any later stage. Cannot start while sleeping or sick.

On screen

Buttons

ButtonAction
AMove your hand and disc to the column on the left (full columns skipped; from the leftmost, round to the rightmost; hold to slide).
BMove your hand and disc to the column on the right (full columns skipped; round from the rightmost; hold to slide).
CDrop your disc down the column.
DPut your hand away to see the board (A, B or C brings it back). Pressed again: CANCEL? YES stops the game (no points).

How to play

The board stands on the table between you: 7 columns, 6 rows. Your discs are solid, the opponent's striped. Who goes first is drawn at random. In turn, drop a disc down a column that isn't full; it falls to the lowest empty hole. Four of yours in a line (across, up and down, or diagonally) wins, and the four flash; a full board with no four is a draw. The opponent always takes a four when it can and blocks yours, and otherwise plays near the middle, but slips up 15% of the time. Points for a win, a few for a draw.
