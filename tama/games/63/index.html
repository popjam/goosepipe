Games

Dots & Boxes

Take turns with another Tamagotchi drawing lines between dots. Close a box to keep it and draw again; more boxes wins.

Playable

Can be drawn when your Tamagotchi becomes an adult or at any later stage. Cannot start while sleeping or sick.

On screen

Buttons

ButtonAction
AThe previous line not yet drawn (hold to slide).
BThe next line not yet drawn (hold to slide).
CDraw the line your hand points at.
DPut your hand away to see the board (A, B or C brings it back). Pressed again: CANCEL? YES stops the game (no points).

How to play

A 4 by 4 board of boxes. Your hand points at a line not yet drawn: A and B step through them row by row, and C draws it. Draw the fourth side of a box and the box is yours (checkered; the opponent's are solid), and you draw again. When every line is drawn, more boxes wins. The opponent takes any box it can and tries not to draw a box's third side, but slips up 15% of the time. Points for a win.

Points

WinPoints earned
Below 1None
1300 points

Points are added when the game ends, before the pile of cash drops. See Points for what they buy.
