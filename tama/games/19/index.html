Games

Shape

Shapes fall down two lanes. Change your shape with B until it matches, then throw it up the left or right lane to knock the falling shape out.

Playable

Can be drawn when your Tamagotchi becomes a teen or at any later stage. Cannot start while sleeping or sick.

On screen

Buttons

ButtonAction
AThrow your shape up the left lane.
BChange your shape: circle, square, triangle, diamond, heart, star.
CThrow your shape up the right lane.
DStop the game and return to the game list (no points).

How to play

Match 30 shapes to win. A throw only hits the lowest shape in its lane: the same shape knocks it out, a different shape bounces off. Only one shape can be in the air at a time. Shapes fall faster as your score rises, and they come in the same order every game. If any shape reaches the bottom line, the game ends.
