Games

Blackjack

Play blackjack for your points against Bill the dealer, with other Tamagotchi at the table. Get closer to 21 than the dealer without going over.

Playable

Can be drawn when your Tamagotchi becomes an adult or at any later stage. Cannot start while sleeping or sick.

On screen

Buttons

ButtonAction
AMove the pointing hand left along the options.
BMove the pointing hand right along the options.
CChoose the option the hand points at. Betting: - (smaller bet), + (bigger bet) or DEAL. Your turn: HIT, STD (stand), DBL (double the bet for one more card) or SPL (split a pair into two hands). After a hand: NEXT.
DPut your hand away to see the table (A, B or C brings it back). Pressed again: CANCEL? YES leaves the table, keeping what you have won (a hand in progress is lost).

How to play

Your points are changed into chips, 10 points to a chip, and each hand's win or loss changes your points straight away. Bet 5, 10, 20, 50 or 100 chips on each hand. Cards 2 to 10 count their number, J Q K count 10 and an ace 1 or 11. The dealer draws to 17 and the other players play their own hands. A win pays your bet, blackjack (an ace and a ten-card as your first two cards) pays 1.5 times it, and a tie gives it back. The game lasts 3 hands, until you cannot cover the smallest bet, or until you leave. At the end your Tamagotchi shows what you won (a bag) or lost (a boot).

Points

This game is played for your points: they are changed into chips when it starts, and each hand you win or lose changes them straight away. There is no points table; at the end a bag shows what you won, or a boot what you lost.
