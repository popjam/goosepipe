Games

Blackjack

Play blackjack for your points against Bill the dealer. Get closer to 21 than the dealer without going over.

Playable

Can be drawn when your Tamagotchi becomes an adult or at any later stage. Cannot start while sleeping or sick.

On screen

Buttons

ButtonAction
ABetting: a smaller bet. Your turn: the previous move (round from the first to the last). After a hand: the next hand.
BBetting: a bigger bet. Your turn: the next move (round from the last to the first). After a hand: the next hand.
CBetting: bet, and your second card turns up. Your turn: make the move shown (HIT, STAND, DOUBLE: double the bet for one more card, SPLIT: a pair into two hands). After a hand: the next hand.
DCANCEL? YES leaves the table, keeping what you have won (a hand in progress is lost).

How to play

You bet your points: each hand's win or loss changes them straight away (your points show at the top right). Each hand you and the dealer get two cards, the first face up and the second face down; you bet 5, 10, 20, 25, 50, 100, 200 or 500 points; then your second card turns up (the dealer's stays down until the dealer plays). Cards 2 to 10 count their number, J Q K count 10 and an ace 1 or 11. The dealer draws to 17. A win pays your bet, blackjack (an ace and a ten-card as your first two cards) pays 1.5 times it, and a tie gives it back. The game lasts 3 hands, until you cannot cover the smallest bet, or until you leave. At the end your Tamagotchi shows what you won (a bag) or lost (a boot).

Points

This game is played for your points: they are changed into chips when it starts, and each hand you win or lose changes them straight away. There is no points table; at the end a bag shows what you won, or a boot what you lost.
