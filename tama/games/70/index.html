Games

Word Shake

Shake 16 letter dice, then find as many words as you can before the clock runs out. Words your opponent also found don't count.

Playable

Can be drawn when your Tamagotchi becomes an adult or at any later stage. Cannot start while sleeping or sick.

On screen

Buttons

ButtonAction
AAt the table: go on. On the results: move the word lists up. Finding words: move to the previous die (with no letters yet, round all 16; then round the dice touching your last letter, anticlockwise).
BAt the table: go on. On the results: move the word lists down. Finding words: move to the next die (round all 16, or clockwise round your last letter).
CAt the table: go on. On the results: go on. Finding words: add the letter (on your last letter: take it off).
DFinding words: try the word. Held: CANCEL? YES stops the game (no points).

How to play

Another Tamagotchi sits across the table with the tray of 16 letter dice; A, B or C and your hand shakes it, then the dice settle in a 4 by 4 grid and the clock starts (180 seconds). Find words by joining neighbouring dice (across, up and down, diagonally), each die once in a word, 3 letters or more. With no letters yet, A / B move round all 16 dice and C picks the first; then A / B go round the free dice touching your last letter and C adds one (C on your last letter takes it off). D tries the word: a new word goes into your list (+points), otherwise NOT A WORD, GOT IT or TOO SHORT. The word list has every common English word (SCOWL, US, British and Australian spellings). Hold D to stop the game. The other player finds words in the same grid at the same time, mostly common ones; both lists show at the end (A / B move them up and down, C goes on). Words you both found score nothing; the rest score by length: 3-4 letters 1, 5 letters 2, 6 letters 3, 7 letters 5, 8 or more 11. The higher total wins. Points for a win.

Points

WinPoints earned
Below 1None
