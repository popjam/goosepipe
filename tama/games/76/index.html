Games

Hurdles

Race another Tamagotchi over ten shared hurdles. Jump to clear them and slam down for a speed boost.

Playable

Can be drawn from adulthood. Cannot start while sleeping or sick.

On screen

Buttons

ButtonAction
AJump from the ground. Hold to rise higher; release to fall. Each jump needs a fresh press.
BSlam down while airborne. Landing gives a fading speed boost.
CNo action.
DStop immediately without points.

How to play

Both runners share one track. A hurdle knocked down stays down, so the runner behind can pass freely. Hitting an upright hurdle slows you to 50% speed for 0.6 s and removes your boost. A slam landing adds 40% speed, fading over 1.5 s. Slamming onto a hurdle causes a stumble. The rival is a different adult, with pace and chances of clipping hurdles drawn each race. The first across the finish line wins 300 points; a tie counts as a loss.
