Games

Manhole

Hearts, money bags and snakes fall towards four manholes, one for each button. Open a hole just as a treat arrives to catch it, but keep it shut when a snake is coming.

Playable

Can be drawn when your Tamagotchi becomes a teen or at any later stage. Cannot start while sleeping or sick.

On screen

Buttons

ButtonAction
AOpen the first hole (left).
BOpen the second hole.
COpen the third hole.
DOpen the fourth hole (right). D does not stop this game.

How to play

Reach 100 to win: a heart is worth 2 and a money bag 3. Closed manholes are white; pressing a button opens its hole (black) for a moment. A caught treat disappears straight away. Every treat must be caught: one that lands on a closed lid ends the game at once, and so does a snake that falls into an open hole (a snake on a closed lid just disappears). Everything speeds up as your score rises: drops fall faster, holes shut sooner, snakes come more often, sometimes right behind a treat, and two things can fall at once. The last stretch from 80 to 100 is meant to be very hard.
