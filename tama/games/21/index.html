Encyclopedia / Flag
Games

Flag

Match the large flag cues by colour. Raise both flags for a double cue, and ignore small fake cues. Reversed positions do not change the colour controls.

Playable

Teen stage onwards; the original TAMA form can also play. Cannot start while sleeping or sick.

On screen

Buttons

ButtonAction
ARaise the black flag during a genuine cue.
BNo action on genuine cues; pressing it during a fake cue still causes a miss.
CRaise the white flag; restart after a miss or completion.
DCancel and return to the game list.

How to play

Eight rounds, three correct cues per round. A + C handles double cues within the chord window. From round three, cues may swap sides and a small fake precedes the second genuine cue. Any A/B/C press during a fake is a miss.
