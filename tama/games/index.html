The games room

Learn the controls, see the screens, and find your next challenge.

How games unlock

Your Tama doesn’t start with every game. Each time it grows into a new stage it is given new games at random, until it has the number for that stage. A game only appears once it has been given, and your Tama can’t be given the same game twice.

StageGames you have by thenNew games that can be given
Baby1—
Child2Jumping Rope, Climb
Teen4Shape, Flag, Manhole
Adult24Memory, Blackjack, Match, Snap, Cup Game, Crazy 8S, Dots & Boxes, Mastermind, Gin Rummy, Connect 4, Pig, War, Word Shake, Spit, Rock Star, Scrabble, Dominion, Word Guess, Hurdles
Elder25—

There are no baby games yet, so babies have none. When there aren’t enough games for a stage, your Tama just has fewer. A new generation starts again with none.

Every game plays out the same way

  • It starts with READY (3 seconds), then GO!! (1 second)
  • When the game ends, its score flashes, then its world record (the best score that game has ever had; NEW WORLD RECORD! when you beat it).
  • Then a pile of cash drops in front of your Tama (a boot if you earned none) and the points you earned flash, then the points you have now.
  • Then a word tells you how well you did.
  • D stops a game at any time, but you get no points for it. (In some games D does something else; in the card games it asks CANCEL? first.)
WordWhen you see it
TOO BADNo score
OKAt least 1% of the top score
GOODAt least 25% of the top score
GREATAt least 50% of the top score
FANTASTICAt least 75% of the top score
PERFECTThe top score

The games

Playable

Jumping Rope

Time your jumps so the rope passes beneath your character. The pace changes as your score increases.

Playable

Climb

Your Tamagotchi stands on a cloud while another drifts back and forth above it. Jump at the right moment to land on it and climb: up through the night into space, down into an alien planet's air, and up to a sky serpent at the top.

Playable

Shape

Shapes fall down two lanes. Change your shape with B until it matches, then throw it up the left or right lane to knock the falling shape out.

Playable

Flag

Match the large flag cues by colour. Raise both flags for a double cue, and ignore small fake cues. Reversed positions do not change the colour controls.

Playable

Manhole

Hearts, money bags and snakes fall towards four manholes, one for each button. Open a hole just as a treat arrives to catch it, but keep it shut when a snake is coming.

Playable

Memory

Watch the pattern of arrows that appears above your Tamagotchi, then repeat it with the buttons. Each round the pattern is one arrow longer.

Playable

Blackjack

Play blackjack for your points against Bill the dealer. Get closer to 21 than the dealer without going over.

Playable

Match

Take turns with another Tamagotchi flipping cards two at a time to find pairs. Find a pair and you go again.

Playable

Snap

Cards turn over one by one onto a pile at the table. When the top two match, slap the pile before the other Tamagotchi does and it's yours.

Playable

Cup Game

Watch the ball go under a cup, follow the cups as they are shuffled on the table, then lift the right one. Ten rounds, each harder than the last; from the seventh, more cups.

Playable

Crazy 8S

Take turns with another Tamagotchi laying cards that match the pile by number or suit. Eights are wild, and black power cards go on anything: draw two, skip, swap hands and more. Be first to empty your hand.

Playable

Dots & Boxes

Take turns with another Tamagotchi drawing lines between dots. Close a box to keep it and draw again; more boxes wins.

Playable

Mastermind

Crack a hidden code of four marbles in eight guesses. Little key pegs tell you how close each guess was.

Playable

Gin Rummy

One hand of Gin Rummy against another Tamagotchi. Take a card, throw one away, and meld your hand into runs and sets; knock when your deadwood is low.

Playable

Connect 4

Take turns with another Tamagotchi dropping discs into an upright board. Four of yours in a row wins.

Playable

Pig

Push your luck with a die against another Tamagotchi. Every roll adds to your turn's total, but the skull loses it all. Bank in time and be first to 50.

Playable

War

Four Tamagotchis share a map. Place your armies, then attack your neighbours with a die for every army. Take every region to win.

Playable

Word Shake

Shake 16 letter dice, then find as many words as you can before the clock runs out. Words your opponent also found don't count.

Playable

Spit

A race with one deck: play a card one up or one down onto either pile before the other player does. First to get rid of every card wins.

Playable

Rock Star

Notes roll down a four-lane highway: press A, B, C or D as each one reaches its target. Keep the streak going and the crowd happy.

Playable

Scrabble

A short game of Scrabble against another Tamagotchi: take turns making words on the board from your 7 tiles. The higher total wins.

Playable

Dominion

Build a deck against another Tamagotchi: play actions, buy treasure and better cards, and end with the most victory points. 10 of the 26 base-set kingdom cards each game.

Playable

Word Guess

Find the hidden five-letter word in six guesses. After each guess, every letter shows whether it is in the right place, somewhere else in the word, or not in it at all.

Playable

Hurdles

Race another Tamagotchi over ten shared hurdles. Jump to clear them and slam down for a speed boost.
