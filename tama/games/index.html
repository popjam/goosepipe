The games room

Learn the controls, see the screens, and find your next challenge.

How games unlock

Your Tama doesn’t start with every game. Each time it grows into a new stage it is given new games at random, until it has the number for that stage. A game only appears once it has been given, and your Tama can’t be given the same game twice.

StageGames you have by thenNew games that can be given
Baby1—
Child2Jumping Rope, Climb
Teen4Shape, Flag, Manhole
Adult10Memory, Blackjack, Match, Snap, Cup Game
Elder11—

There are no baby games yet, so babies have none. When there aren’t enough games for a stage, your Tama just has fewer. A new generation starts again with none.

Every game plays out the same way

  • It starts with READY (3 seconds), then GO!! (1 second)
  • When the game ends, a pile of cash drops in front of your Tama and the points you earned flash on the screen.
  • Then a word tells you how well you did.
  • D stops a game at any time, but you get no points for it. (In Manhole, Memory and Blackjack, D is a game button, so they play to the end.)
WordWhen you see it
TOO BADNo score
OKAt least 1% of the top score
GOODAt least 25% of the top score
GREATAt least 50% of the top score
FANTASTICAt least 75% of the top score
PERFECTThe top score

The games

Playable

Jumping Rope

Time your jumps so the rope passes beneath your character. The pace changes as your score increases.

Playable

Climb

Your Tamagotchi stands on a cloud while another drifts back and forth above it. Jump at the right moment to land on it and climb higher.

Playable

Shape

Shapes fall down two lanes. Change your shape with B until it matches, then throw it up the left or right lane to knock the falling shape out.

Playable

Flag

Match the large flag cues by colour. Raise both flags for a double cue, and ignore small fake cues. Reversed positions do not change the colour controls.

Playable

Manhole

Hearts, money bags and snakes fall towards four manholes, one for each button. Open a hole just as a treat arrives to catch it, but keep it shut when a snake is coming.

Playable

Memory

Watch the pattern of arrows that appears above your Tamagotchi, then repeat it with the buttons. Each round the pattern is one arrow longer.

Playable

Blackjack

Play blackjack for your points against Bill the dealer, with other Tamagotchi at the table. Get closer to 21 than the dealer without going over.

Playable

Match

Take turns with another Tamagotchi flipping cards two at a time to find pairs. Find a pair and you go again.

Playable

Snap

Take turns with another Tamagotchi laying cards on one pile. When the top two cards match, be first to call SNAP and the pile is yours.

Playable

Cup Game

Watch the ball go under a cup, follow the cups as they are shuffled on the table, then lift the right one. Five rounds, each faster than the last.
