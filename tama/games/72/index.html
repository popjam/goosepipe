Rock Star

Notes roll down a four-lane highway: press A, B, C or D as each one reaches its target. Keep the streak going and the crowd happy.

Playable

Can be drawn when your Tamagotchi becomes an adult or at any later stage. Cannot start while sleeping or sick.

On screen

Buttons

ButtonAction
APlay the left lane (hold it through a note's tail). On the results: go on.
BPlay the second lane. On the results: go on.
CPlay the third lane. On the results: go on.
DPlay the right lane. Held for 2 seconds with no D note near: CANCEL? YES stops the game (no points).

How to play

Four lanes roll down a highway towards a row of targets marked A, B, C and D. Notes roll down at one steady speed. Press a lane's button while its note touches the target (from first touch to last: 0.18 seconds either side of the middle). Two notes side by side are a chord: press both. A note with a tail: keep its button down until the tail has passed, for more points. 10 notes in a row double the points, 20 triple, 30 quadruple. A missed note, or a press with no note there, breaks the streak and lowers the meter at the right; empty, the song fails. Songs are made up as they play (50 seconds), with more notes towards the end. At the end, the share of notes hit gives up to 5 stars. Points by stars.
