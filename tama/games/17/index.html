Games

Jumping Rope

Time your jumps so the rope passes beneath your character. The pace changes as your score increases.

Playable

Can be drawn when your Tamagotchi becomes a child or at any later stage. Cannot start while sleeping or sick.

On screen

Buttons

ButtonAction
AJump during play.
BJump during play.
CJump during play.
DStop the game and return to the game list (no points).

How to play

A, B and C do nothing during READY and GO!! A new jump cannot start until the current jump finishes. The rope crosses at 75% of its turn.

Points

JumpsPoints earned
Below 1None
1–410 points
5–1050 points

Points are added when the game ends, before the pile of cash drops. See Points for what they buy.
