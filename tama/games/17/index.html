Games

Jumping Rope

Time your jumps so the rope passes beneath your character. The pace changes as your score increases.

Playable

Child stage onwards; the original TAMA form can also play. Cannot start while sleeping or sick.

On screen

Buttons

ButtonAction
AJump during play.
BJump during play.
CJump during play; restart after a miss or completion.
DCancel and return to the game list.

How to play

A, B and C do nothing during the ready countdown. A new jump cannot start until the current jump finishes. The rope crosses at 75% of its turn.
