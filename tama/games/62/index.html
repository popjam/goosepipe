Games

Crazy 8S

Take turns with another Tamagotchi laying cards that match the pile by number or suit. Eights are wild. Be first to empty your hand.

Playable

Can be drawn when your Tamagotchi becomes an adult or at any later stage. Cannot start while sleeping or sick.

On screen

Buttons

ButtonAction
AMove through your hand (hold to slide), and past its end to the deck. After an 8: the previous suit.
BMove through your hand (hold to slide), and past its end to the deck. After an 8: the next suit.
CPlay the card shown big, draw from the deck, or (having drawn) pass. After an 8: name the suit.
DPut your hand away to see the table (A, B or C brings it back). Pressed again: CANCEL? YES stops the game (no points).

How to play

Each player is dealt 7 cards and one card is turned up to start the pile. In turn, play a card matching the top of the pile by number or suit. An 8 can be played on anything, and whoever plays it names the suit to follow. If you can't (or would rather not) play, point at the deck to draw one card: you may play that card, or point at the deck again to pass. When the deck runs out, the pile is shuffled back in under its top card. First to empty their hand wins. Score: 10 for winning, plus the cards left in the opponent's hand.
